# Fast-Publication Venue Strategy (10-Day Window)

## Important
Guaranteed acceptance is not possible in peer review. This plan maximizes acceptance probability by aligning scope, rigor, and timeline.

## Priority Submission Path
1. Applied workshop or symposium track in AI for healthcare / NLP for social good with short cycle decisions.
2. Regional conference short paper or poster track with practical-impact emphasis.
3. Preprint release immediately after submission where venue policy allows.

## Why This Paper Can Be Competitive Quickly
1. Clear practical problem: domain shift in mental-health NLP.
2. Strong and simple core result: external performance recovery under strict adaptation.
3. Reproducible artifacts already available in the repository.
4. Claim discipline already aligned with reviewer expectations.

## What to Finalize Before Submission
1. Replace all author placeholders and affiliations.
2. Verify all references and bibliography metadata.
3. Add one page of error analysis with representative examples.
4. Add confidence intervals for external metrics if possible.
5. Ensure manuscript matches exact venue template length and section order.

## Submission Bundle Checklist
1. Main manuscript PDF.
2. Source manuscript and bibliography files.
3. Supplementary reproducibility appendix.
4. Ethics and limitations statement.
5. Cover letter customized to target venue.
